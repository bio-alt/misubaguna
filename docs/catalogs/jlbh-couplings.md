## p004 — 企业介绍 (Company Introduction, Chinese)

- **Page type:** company intro
- **Layout:** title "企业介绍" at the top left with a blue underline, five paragraphs of Chinese text, and a full-width photo across the bottom half: the factory gate and guard house, a grey/white industrial hall with an orange stripe, flagpoles, a blue sky, and a yellow-hatched driveway.
- **Full translation:**
  - Julu County Binhao Transmission Equipment Co., Ltd. was **founded in 2015** and is located in the **Julu County Industrial Park, Xingtai, Hebei**. It is a high-tech enterprise that does R&D, design, production and sales. The company covers **30 mu** (~2 ha), has **12,000 m² of standardized workshops**, has **180+ sets of CNC machining and precision testing equipment**, and currently employs **130+ staff**. It can produce at scale to standardized processes.
  - The company makes a wide range of power-transmission products. Its main lines are **star (星型), plum-blossom (梅花), gear (齿式), barrel/drum-gear (鼓形齿) and diaphragm (膜片) couplings, plus bell housings (钟型罩) and rubber elastic pads (橡胶弹性垫)**. There are **20+ series and 200+ products**, suited to many industrial operating conditions and to custom requirements. The "Binhao" (斌昊) brand has stable quality and reliable performance, and has a good reputation and high recognition in the industry.
  - The company has an **automated coupling test center** and applies full-process quality control. Its products are widely used in **metallurgy, mining, electric power, petroleum, chemical, marine/shipbuilding and general machinery**. Its sales network covers most provinces and cities of China, and it exports to **Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe and America**.
  - The company is committed to technical innovation. It co-founded an **A-grade coupling technology research center with universities** and holds **14 self-developed patents**. Its business philosophy is "Quality first, service first, timeliness first". It has built a modern management system and an efficient production, sales and R&D team. Honors received: **National High-tech Enterprise; Hebei Province Manufacturing Single-Item Champion; "Specialized, Refined, Distinctive and Innovative" (专精特新) SME; Innovative SME; Technology-based SME; Advanced-level Smart Factory**. It is **ISO 9001 certified**.
  - Future: the company will keep deepening its core coupling business, strengthen R&D and quality control, expand in global markets, and aim to become an internationally competitive specialist manufacturer of transmission equipment.
